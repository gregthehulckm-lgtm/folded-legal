ADDING AND EDITING PIECES

Adding a piece

Go to Closet → ＋ ADD, or tap Add your first piece on the Today screen. Photograph the garment or choose a photo from your library, then give it a name, a colour and a fabric. Lay the piece flat and fill the frame — it photographs best that way.

Choosing colour and fabric

Pick from the palette, or tap the dashed ＋ swatch to mix your own colour and name it whatever you like. For fabric, choose a preset, use MIX for blends such as 65% cotton, 35% linen, or CUSTOM to type anything at all.

Changing a piece later

Open the piece from your closet. Tap the colour swatch or the FABRIC panel to change either — both say EDIT. Last worn and Wears can be typed into directly, and WORE IT updates them for you in one tap.

Removing a piece

Open it and tap Remove from closet, or press and hold its row in the Closet list. You will be asked to confirm. Removing a piece cannot be undone.

WEARING AND PLANNING

Today's outfit

The Today screen proposes an outfit from what you own, taking the weather and today's calendar into account. Tap ↻ AGAIN for another suggestion, or WEAR THIS to log it as worn. If you have not granted location or calendar access, suggestions still work — they simply have less to go on.

Asking for something specific

Tap Ask for something else and type the way you would say it aloud: “something black for a warm night out”. Folded reads it as colour, fabric, weather and occasion, and answers with what your closet actually holds.

Looks

Build an outfit from a piece (Use in a look) or from a search (Dress me), name it, and save it. Saved looks live in the Looks tab and can be worn again in one tap.

Wanted

For pieces you are eyeing but do not own. Add a photo, a link, or both, with a description and a price. Nothing here counts towards your closet until you add it properly.

SHARING AND BACKING UP

Sharing your closet

Closet → SHARE sets your wardrobe as a receipt — every piece, its colour, fabric and wear count — and shows it to you before anything is sent. Tap Send to a friend to share it by message, mail or AirDrop.

Backing up

Settings → Back up my closet creates a zip holding a spreadsheet of every piece plus all of your photographs, then hands it to the share sheet so you can save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.

Please back up. Folded keeps everything on your phone and nothing on our servers. That is good for privacy, but it means deleting the app deletes your closet permanently, and it will not reappear on a new phone. Export a backup every so often, and before you change devices.

SETTINGS

  • Wear reminders — a nudge to log what you wore, on Sunday evenings or every evening. Turn it off any time.
  • Currency — sets the symbol used for prices on your Wanted list. Changing it re-labels every price.
  • Colour — six themes that recolour the whole app. Your clothes always sit on a light card so photographs never shift.

PERMISSIONS

Folded asks for the camera and photo library when you add a piece, location for the weather, calendar for today's plans, and notifications for reminders. All are optional and the app works without them. To change your mind later, open iOS Settings → Folded.

COMMON PROBLEMS

The camera does not open

Check iOS Settings → Folded and confirm Camera and Photos are allowed.

The weather looks wrong

Folded uses your location for local conditions. If location is denied it falls back to a default, so suggestions will not match the day. Allow location in iOS Settings → Folded.

Reminders are not arriving

Reminders need notification permission. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Folded, then set the reminder again in Folded's settings.

My closet looks empty

A colour or category filter may still be applied in the Closet tab. Tap ALL, and tap the selected colour swatch again to clear it.

PRIVACY

Your photographs and everything about your wardrobe stay on your device. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
