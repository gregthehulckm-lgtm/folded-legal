Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 11 SEPTEMBER 2026

Folded is a wardrobe kept on your phone. Almost nothing you put into it ever leaves the device.

THE SHORT VERSION

There are no accounts, no servers of ours, and no analytics. Your photographs, your clothes, your saved looks and the details you enter during setup are stored on your phone and nowhere else. Only two things ever leave the device: your approximate location, to fetch the weather, and — only if you choose to join the Folded letter — your email address, sent to the service that delivers it.

WHAT FOLDED STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your setup details — the name and date of birth you enter when you first open the app. If you give an email address, a copy is kept here too so the app can show you what you joined with.
  • Your closet — photographs of your clothes, along with their names, colours, fabrics, tags, wear counts and dates.
  • Your looks and your Wanted list — saved outfits, and any photos, links, descriptions and prices you add.
  • Your preferences — colour theme, currency and reminder settings.

All of this lives in the app's own storage on your phone. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. We cannot see any of it.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

Weather. If you allow location access, Folded sends your coordinates to Open-Meteo to look up the current temperature and conditions, so outfit suggestions match the day. The request contains only those coordinates. No name, no device identifier, nothing else. If you deny location access, Folded uses a default and everything else keeps working.

The Folded letter. If you join the monthly letter — during setup, or later from Settings — your email address is sent to Kit, the service we use to deliver it. Kit stores it on our behalf and uses it for nothing except sending you the letter; their handling of it is covered by Kit's privacy policy. Only the address is sent. Your name, birthday and everything about your wardrobe stay on the phone. If you skip the letter, nothing is sent at all.

That is the complete list. Folded's typefaces are bundled inside the app rather than fetched from a font service, so nothing else is contacted when you open it, and the app works with no signal at all.

PERMISSIONS AND WHY

  • Camera and photo library — to photograph a garment, or pick an existing photo, when you add a piece. Photos you choose stay in the app on your device.
  • Location — used only for the weather lookup described above.
  • Calendar — Folded reads the titles of today's events so suggestions suit your plans. Event data is read on the device, used immediately, and never stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on wear reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone; no push server is involved.

Every one of these is optional. Decline any of them and the rest of the app still works.

SHARING AND EXPORTING

When you share a closet receipt or export a backup, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Folded does not send it anywhere on your behalf.

WHAT FOLDED DOES NOT DO

  • No accounts, logins or profiles held by us.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. The only thing we ever hold is the email address of people who asked for the letter, and it is used for the letter alone.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Deleting a piece removes it from your closet immediately. Deleting the app from your phone removes everything Folded has stored, permanently. None of that ever reached us, so there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to erase.

The one exception is the letter. Every letter carries an unsubscribe link, which removes your address from the list straight away. If you would rather we delete it entirely, write to the address below and we will.

CHILDREN

Folded is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As above, no personal information reaches us in any case.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app.
